# Portfolio images

Put your portfolio images in this folder. JPG and PNG files are fine; WebP is recommended when convenient because it usually makes smaller downloads. Keep the longest edge at 1600 px or less and aim for under 300 KB per image. Do not upload video files here. Use the suggested lowercase, hyphenated names and keep the appropriate file extension (for example, `.jpg` or `.png`) if you are not converting to WebP.

Suggested dimensions:

- Video thumbnails: 1280×720 px (16:9).
- Other card thumbnails: about 1200 px wide; 4:3 or square is fine.
- NU Wizards Circle, social media, and photography gallery images: around 1000–1600 px on the longest edge. Preserve their original aspect ratios; portrait screenshots/post designs are welcome.
- Link preview image (`og-image`): 1200×630 px.

These are recommendations, not upload requirements. Keep original proportions and send the images in their existing formats if that is easiest; they can be resized or converted when wired into the site.
