A little about me

About

I'm a fourth-year BS Information Technology student specializing in Multimedia Arts and Animation at National University - Manila. I work as a Graphic Designer for NU Wizards Circle, where I've produced publication materials and social media graphics for two campus esports tournaments and designed print ID cards that were manufactured and used at an event.

My work spans video editing, audio post-production, graphic design, and layout. I'm currently building my portfolio and open to multimedia and graphic design internships where I can contribute to a creative team and keep learning.

Tools: Adobe Photoshop, CapCut, Audacity, Cakewalk Next, Figma, Canva, Blender
