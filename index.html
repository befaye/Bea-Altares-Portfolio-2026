About

I'm a 4th-year BS Information Technology student specializing in Multimedia Arts and Animation at National University - Manila. My work spans graphic design and layout, video and animation, and audio production, mostly through campus organizations, coursework, and personal projects. A lot of what I've learned came from designing for NU Wizards Circle, where I produced campaign materials for two campus esports tournaments, print collateral for the Estudyante Esports Caravan, and ID cards for organization members.

Most projects end up needing more than one thing. A campaign might need a poster, a post, and a short video, and I'd rather be able to make all three than hand off two of them. That's part of why I'm currently learning After Effects, to go deeper on the motion side.
